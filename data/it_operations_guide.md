# Northstar Enterprise Services IT Operations Guide
This document contains fictional training content for the IT Service & Asset Operations Assistant.

## Laptop Replacement Policy
Devices may be considered for replacement when they are more than four years old, experience repeated hardware failures, or can no longer support approved business software. Employees should submit a replacement request through the service desk. Endpoint Support will review the asset condition and replacement eligibility.

## Incident Priority Guide
High Priority:
Issues causing major business disruption, critical outages, security concerns, or widespread impact.

Medium Priority:
Problems affecting a user or service that still has a temporary workaround.

Low Priority:
Minor issues with limited impact, cosmetic problems, or requests that do not disrupt normal business operations.

## VPN Troubleshooting
Before contacting Network Support, verify internet connectivity, restart the VPN client, confirm credentials are correct, and reconnect. If the issue continues after these checks, contact Network Support and include any error messages.

## Lost or Stolen Device Procedure
Lost or stolen devices must be reported immediately to the service desk and the employee's manager. Endpoint Support and Security teams must be notified for investigation and risk assessment.

## Software Installation Rules
Only approved software may be installed on company devices. Employees must request approval before installing software not included in the approved software catalog. Administrative permissions should not be granted without authorization.

## Ticket Escalation Process
Tickets should be escalated when service targets are at risk, business impact increases, or specialized support is required. Support teams must document troubleshooting performed before escalation. Escalated tickets should include current status, impact assessment, and supporting evidence.
